# ÆZ Arena — Gold Liquid Glass Redesign

Added `aez-gold-glass.css` as a final visual layer. It preserves the existing HTML/JS/Supabase logic while introducing a premium iOS-inspired glass system, warm gold accent palette, separate light/dark themes, responsive polish, accessible focus states, and reduced-motion support.

The stylesheet is loaded after the existing design styles so the redesign can be reverted by removing its single `<link>` from `index.html`.
